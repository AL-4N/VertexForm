Five exercises, each measured its own way.

A squat is judged on how low your thighs get and how far you lean; a push-up on your elbow angle and whether your body stays in one line. Pull-ups and the L-sit are next. Open any exercise to see it in 3D.

Squat

Side-on

Thigh angle to the ground, torso lean, how steady you hold the bottom.

Flags: cutting depth, folding forward, knees drifting, bouncing.

Push-up

Side-on

Elbow angle at the bottom and the line from shoulders to ankles.

Flags: half reps, sagging hips, piking, dropping the head.

Plank

Hold

How far your hips drift off a straight body line.

Flags: sagging, piking, swaying.

Lunge

Side-on

Front-knee angle and how upright you stay.

Flags: shallow depth, leaning over the front leg.

Jumping jack

Front-on

How high your hands get and how wide your feet go.

Flags: short arms, lazy feet.

From camera to coaching in four steps.

All of it happens on your device, on every frame from your camera.

  1. 01

    It finds you

    A pose model running in your browser places 33 points on your body in every frame of the webcam feed.

  2. 02

    It measures angles

    Points become joint angles. An angle is the same whether you're tall or short, close to the camera or far away.

  3. 03

    It grades the rep

    When a rep finishes, it's scored across the whole bottom of the movement, split into parts like depth and posture.

    It tells you

Common mistakes

Coach says

How the scoring is tested.

Every grade sits on one scale, from coral (F) to green (A). The scoring was tested on simulated reps with known angles, to make sure good form scores high and shortcuts don't.

  • One bad frame can't ruin a rep. A rep's score is the median of every frame at the bottom of the movement.
  • Bouncing doesn't count. A rep needs real time at the bottom, and quick rebounds are flagged.
  • It won't guess. Frames where your joints are hidden or out of shot are thrown out, and you're told to adjust the camera.
  • Parallel means parallel. Depth is the thigh's angle to the floor, the definition a coach would use.

It talks, so you don't have to read mid‑rep.

After every rep you hear your score and the one fix that matters most. The wording changes from rep to rep so you don't hear the same line every time, and you choose the voice it coaches in.

What stays on your device

The pose model runs in your browser and no video is ever uploaded. There's no account to make. Your scores, bests and rep replays are saved in this browser only.

What it can't do

  • A side-on camera can't see knees caving inward or weight shifting side to side.
  • Cut-off feet or a dark room hurt tracking. When that happens, it warns you and holds back the score.
  • It compares you to general coaching ranges. For pain or an injury, see a physio.
