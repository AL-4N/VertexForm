Coach says

From camera to coaching in four steps.

All of it happens on your device, about thirty times a second.

  1. 1

    It finds you

    A pose model running in your browser places 33 points on your body in every frame of the webcam feed.

  2. 2

    It measures angles

    Points become joint angles. An angle is the same whether you're tall or short, close to the camera or far away.

  3. 3

    It grades the rep

    When a rep finishes, it's scored across the whole bottom of the movement, split into parts like depth and posture.

  4. 4

    It tells you

    You hear the score and the single most important fix, then go again.

A score that means something.

Every grade sits on one scale, from coral to green. The engine was tested on exact synthetic movements to make sure good form scores high and shortcuts don't.

  • One bad frame can't ruin a rep. Scores use the median across every frame at the bottom, not a single snapshot.
  • It won't guess. Frames where your joints are hidden or out of shot are thrown out, and you're told to adjust the camera.
  • Parallel means parallel. Depth is the thigh's angle to the floor, the definition a coach would use.

Private by design.

  • The pose model runs on your device. No video is uploaded, ever.

Honest about its limits.

  • A side-on camera can't see knees caving inward or weight shifting side to side.
  • Cut-off feet or a dark room hurt tracking. You'll get a warning, not a fake score.
  • It compares you to general coaching ranges. It isn't a physio.
