          Tracking model
          
      
            
            
            
            
          
      
        
      
        
      Auto starts on Balanced and switches to Fast if your device can't keep up.
          Max accuracy downloads a 31 MB model and needs a decent graphics chip.
      
      
      

      
      
        
      Help and shortcuts
      
        
      
          
      Set up
      
          
      Camera at hip height, 2–3 m away, side-on (face it for jumping jacks), light in front of you.
            A dashed box shows where your whole body should be until you're set.
      
          
      Camera shows nothing
      
          
      Pick another camera in the Camera list (here or on the live screen). Virtual cameras (OBS, Camo…)
            are skipped unless you pick one. "Camera busy" means another tab or app has it: close other tabs of
            this site, video-call apps and browser sidebar apps.
      
          
      Reps not counting
      
          
      Start from the top position and hold still for a moment so it can learn your body. Keep your
            whole body in view. Standing still at the top for 4 s counts as a rest: the set clock pauses.
      
          
      Live screen keys
      
          
      Space pause · M mute · F fullscreen · G gym mode ·
            Esc back. Every key has a button too.
      
          
      Your data
      
          
      Everything stays in this browser: no uploads, no account. History → Export CSV saves a copy.
            Works offline after your first session; install it from your browser's menu.

      1. Camera side-on, at hip height
      
      
      Put your laptop or phone level with your hips and stand side-on to it. For jumping jacks, face it instead.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
      2. Step back 2–3 metres
      
      
      Your whole body, head to toe, needs to be in the picture with a little space around it. The app shows a guide box and tells you which way to move.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
      3. Light in front of you
      
      
      Face a window or lamp, not away from it. A bright window behind you turns you into a silhouette and makes tracking guess.
